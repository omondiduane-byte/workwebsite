### Chama Deals
- Definition：A bulk buying feature where multiple backers pool funds to purchase goods at a discounted price; tracks total price, portion price, target portions, and filled portions with an array of backers.
- Aliases：chama_deals、bulk buying

### Gas-O-Meter
- Definition：A smart gas tracking feature that monitors household gas usage, predicts remaining days, and suggests refill timing based on consumption patterns.
- Aliases：gas_predictions、gas tracker

### Escrow Transactions
- Definition：A payment holding system that temporarily holds buyer funds until delivery is confirmed, then releases them to vendors or refunds buyers if issues arise.
- Aliases：escrow、holding payments

### Boda Boda Rider Fleet
- Definition：The motorcycle delivery service component that manages rider assignments, job claiming, OTP verification, and delivery status tracking.
- Aliases：delivery_jobs、rider dispatch

### Vendor Approvals Queue
- Definition：A registration workflow where new vendor shop applications are reviewed, approved, and assigned login credentials for self-onboarding into the marketplace.
- Aliases：vendor_approvals、shop registration

### Rider Approvals Queue
- Definition：A registration workflow where new boda boda riders submit their details and vehicle information for approval before being assigned delivery jobs.
- Aliases：rider_approvals、rider onboarding

### Inquiries System
- Definition：A customer support feature allowing users to submit questions or complaints, which can be answered by administrators with status tracking.
- Aliases：inquiries、help desk、support tickets

### Banned Vendors List
- Definition：A blacklist table storing names of vendors that have been suspended or banned from the marketplace platform.
- Aliases：banned_vendors、vendor blacklist
